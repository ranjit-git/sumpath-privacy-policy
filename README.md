# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we ("the Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collect, use, and share information in connection with your use of our mobile application **SumPath: Math Puzzle Game** (the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our App. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

SumPath: Math puzzle Game is designed to respect your privacy. **We do not require you to create an account, nor do we ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.**

However, to provide, support, and improve the App, we use third-party services that may automatically collect certain information.

### A. Automatically Collected Information
When you use the App, third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain information automatically, including:
*   **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), and mobile network information.
*   **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the App, such as time spent in the App, screens visited, levels completed, and in-game economy interactions (e.g., earning or spending hints).
*   **Diagnostic Data:** Crash logs, performance data, and other technical information if the App encounters an error.

### B. Local Data Storage
Your game progress (such as highest level unlocked, collected gems, and hints) is saved locally on your device's internal storage. We do not upload, sync, or store your game save data on our servers. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this local progress will be deleted.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services have their own Privacy Policies.

*   **Google Analytics for Firebase:** Used to understand how users interact with the App (funnel analysis, level completion rates) so we can improve game design and difficulty. [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)
*   **Firebase Crashlytics:** Used to identify and fix bugs and crashes in the App. [Crashlytics Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
*   **Google AdMob:** Used to serve advertisements within the App. AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve personalized ads based on your interests. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   **Google Play Billing:** If you choose to make in-app purchases (e.g., buying hints or removing ads), the transaction is securely processed by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or financial information.

## 3. GDPR, CCPA, and User Consent

We strictly adhere to global privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

*   **User Messaging Platform (UMP):** When you first launch the App in a regulated region (such as the EEA/UK), you will be presented with a consent form regarding the collection of data for personalized advertising. 
*   **Revoking Consent:** You have the right to change your mind at any time. You can view or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time by navigating to the **Settings** menu within the App and tapping the **Privacy Options** button.
